# Returns Policy

Trailhead Supply is a fictional outdoor gear store used for this demo. All policies below are made up.

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with all tags attached.

Items marked "final sale" cannot be returned. Gift cards are not refundable.

## How to start a return

Sign in to your account, open the order, and choose "Start a return". Print the prepaid label and drop the parcel at any partner courier point. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the parcel.

## Return shipping costs

Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items. For all other returns, a flat shipping fee of $6 is deducted from the refund.
