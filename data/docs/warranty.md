# Warranty

Trailhead Supply is a fictional store. These warranty terms are invented for the demo.

## What is covered

Backpacks, tents and sleeping bags carry a 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Footwear carries a 1-year warranty.

## What is not covered

Normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, and damage caused by washing items against the care label are not covered.

## Making a claim

Email support with your order number and clear photos of the problem. We reply within 2 business days. If the claim is approved we repair, replace or refund the item, at our choice.

## Repairs after the warranty ends

Out-of-warranty zipper and seam repairs are available for a flat fee of $20 per item.
