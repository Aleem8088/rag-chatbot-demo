# Shipping FAQ

Trailhead Supply is a fictional store. Delivery details below are invented for the demo.

## Delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $15. Orders placed before 2 PM local warehouse time ship the same day.

## Free shipping

Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping within the contiguous United States.

## International orders

We currently ship to Canada and the United Kingdom only. International orders take 7 to 12 business days and customers pay any import duties and taxes.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed as soon as your parcel leaves the warehouse. If you have not received it within 48 hours of ordering, contact support.
